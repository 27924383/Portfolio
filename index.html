04 / process

How it goes.

  1. Send me the task, your timeline and your budget. DM me or reply to the post.

  2. I tell you if I can take it and what it costs. Payment amount is always negotiable.

  3. You pay the down payment and I start. If we're doing per-task and you forget something, or heavily change your mind on work that's already been done and reviewed, I charge a bit extra for that.

  4. You get the code. Readable, no libraries you have to keep alive, and any scripter can pick it up after me.
