01 / services

What I actually like working on.

I'm not trying to be the guy you hire for fifty unrelated tiny scripts. I prefer complete systems with a clear job.

Gameplay systems

Combat, movement, abilities, round systems, towers, enemies, NPC behaviour and other mechanics that need more than a few disconnected scripts.

Networking & replication

Client/server architecture, state replication, remote design, validation and systems that need to stay responsive without trusting the client.

Backend systems

Data, inventories, progression, currencies, player state and the code that has to keep the rest of the game from turning into a mess.

NPCs & simulation

AI behaviour, targeting, path logic, entity-heavy systems and anything where performance starts mattering once there are a lot of things running.

Frameworks & APIs

Reusable modules, state machines, internal APIs and project-specific infrastructure when a normal one-off script isn't enough.

Developer tooling

Plugins, utilities and tools that make development faster or remove repetitive work from the rest of a project.

03 / fit

The kind of commission I want.

This saves both of us time.

Good fit

You need a new system built from scratch and you know what it should do.

You want me to own the technical side of that system instead of micromanaging individual scripts.

The job involves gameplay, architecture, networking, backend logic, simulation or tooling.

Probably not for me

Most of the work is cleaning up a large codebase made by somebody else.

You need UI design, animation, models, building, VFX or audio. I script; I don't pretend to do everything.

You only have payment through Robux, crypto or revenue share.

04 / process

How I work.

  1. Send me what you want built, your budget and your timeline. A video, reference game or rough spec helps more than a giant paragraph.

  2. I tell you whether I want the job, what I think the scope actually is and what I'd charge for it.

  3. Once the down payment is handled, I build the system and keep the code structured around the job instead of stacking patches until it works.

  4. You get readable code and a system another competent scripter can continue working with later.

05 / about

About me.

I'm jub, lead programmer at KMAP Studios. I've been scripting on Roblox for 6+ years. I also have a technical degree in quality and study robotics and advanced computing.

I like knowing how the systems I work with actually function. I don't build around a framework just because it's popular, and I don't want a project held together by code nobody understands. If custom architecture makes sense, I'll build it. If something simpler does the job, I'll use the simpler thing.

Have a system in mind?

Send me the task, budget and timeline on Discord. Payment is through PayPal. I don't take Robux, crypto or pure revenue-share deals.
